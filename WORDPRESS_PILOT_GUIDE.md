## 🚀 Quick Start (10 Minutes to First Publish)

### **Step 1: Connect Your WordPress Site (5 minutes)**

1. **Log into Orion** with your pilot credentials
2. **Navigate to Settings** → **WordPress Integration**
3. **Add your WordPress site:**
   - **Site URL:** `https://yoursite.com` (full URL)
   - **Username:** Your WordPress username (not email)
   - **App Password:** Generate in WordPress admin (see below)

#### **Creating WordPress App Password:**
1. Go to your WordPress admin dashboard
2. Navigate to **Users** → **Profile**
3. Scroll down to **"Application Passwords"**
4. Enter **"Orion Pilot"** as the application name
5. Click **"Add New Application Password"**
6. **Copy the generated password** (you won't see it again!)
7. Paste it into Orion's WordPress connection form

4. **Test the connection** - you should see your site name and WordPress version
5. **Save the connection** - you're ready to publish!

### **Step 2: Create Your First Content (5 minutes)**

1. **Go to Content** → **Topics**
2. **Create a new topic set:**
   - Enter 3-5 topic ideas for your site
   - Select your target keywords
   - Choose content categories
3. **Generate drafts** - Orion will create AI-powered content
4. **Review and approve** drafts that meet your quality standards
5. **Publish to WordPress** with one click!

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### **WordPress Connection Problems**
- **"Connection Failed"** → Check your site URL and app password
- **"Unauthorized"** → Verify your WordPress username and app password
- **"Site Not Found"** → Ensure your WordPress site is accessible
- **"API Disabled"** → Contact your hosting provider to enable REST API

### **Publishing Issues**
- **"Publish Failed"** → Check WordPress connection and try again
- **"Quality Score Too Low"** → Address rulebook violations first
- **"Permission Denied"** → Ensure your WordPress user has publish permissions
- **"Category Not Found"** → Create missing categories in WordPress first

### **Content Quality Issues**
- **"Rulebook Failed"** → Review violations and suggestions
- **"Low Quality Score"** → Improve content based on AI recommendations
- **"Missing Keywords"** → Add target keywords to title and content
- **"Poor Readability"** → Simplify sentences and improve structure
